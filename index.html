FactorGrad-GSExact-Primal Monte Carlo Structural Backpropagation for Efficient 3D Gaussian Splatting

Keep the structural loss value exact. Sample the structural gradient where it costs the most.

FactorGrad-GS retains dense ℓ1 supervision and the exact D-SSIM forward value, while sampling D-SSIM derivatives across color channels and training iterations.

Method

Exact forward, sampled backward

One rendering pass supplies the image pair and the exact scalar loss. FactorGrad-GS spends fewer backward operations on structural terms while preserving dense ℓ1 gradients.

01 / Forward

Preserve the objective

Compute the full RGB structural value with a selective fused-SSIM path, so the host gate receives the exact scalar objective.

02 / Backward

Sample structural derivatives

Use inverse-probability-weighted color-channel and temporal sampling for D-SSIM derivatives, alongside dense ℓ1 supervision.

03 / Schedule

Respect densification

Use channel-only sampling during growth, restore full RGB after densification, then enable temporal sampling during late refinement.

Reviewer follow-up

Supplementary Experiments

We reran the matched protocol to measure seed sensitivity, sampling-policy effects, D-SSIM weight sensitivity, and a small high-resolution subset. All runs use FastGS Base with SkipGS as the matched host, 30K iterations, densification interval 500, fixed train/test cameras, and the reported FactorGrad-GS schedule.

Multi-seed robustness (P0)

Across 13 scenes and host seeds 42, 2026, and 3407, FactorGrad-GS reduces end-to-end wall time by 3.70% (2526.94 vs. 2623.93 s) and CUDA time by 4.37%, with near-unchanged FPS (+1.03) and a small quality trade-off (−0.0457 PSNR, −0.00054 SSIM, +0.00111 LPIPS). These results support a speed–quality trade-off rather than a claim of lossless acceleration.

IID vs. cycle: +0.01188 PSNR, +0.000207 SSIM, −0.000139 LPIPS, and +0.80% time saving over the cycle policy across all 13 scenes. The control is consistent with nearly unchanged quality.

High-resolution subset

At 2400 px on Bicycle, Kitchen, and Counter, FactorGrad-GS saves 5.18% wall time with ΔPSNR −0.04934, ΔSSIM −0.000780, and ΔLPIPS +0.000993 versus SkipGS.

This is a three-scene subset, so it should not be generalized to the full benchmark.

P0 quality, FPS, VRAM, and Gaussian-count entries are scene means; wall/CUDA time is summed over scenes. The complete reviewer protocol and detailed per-scene statistics are kept with the project records.
